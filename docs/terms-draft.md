## Account, encryption and recovery

The SSH key proves access; the local passphrase protects archived messages. The offline recovery code replaces a lost SSH key but cannot open an archive whose passphrase is lost or restore messages never decrypted on the device.

Compare fingerprints through another trusted channel before verifying contacts and after identity changes. Encryption does not protect a compromised device, unlocked copy or delivery metadata. Pseudonymity does not guarantee absolute anonymity.

Development/cybersecurity discussions, including authorized research, are allowed. Harassment, impersonation, spam, illegal distribution and unauthorized attacks are not. The future operator may restrict/suspend access for substantiated abuse and provide review through `[PUBLIC EMAIL]`.

The operator does not possess readable direct-chat content from official clients. Never request private keys/passphrases to investigate a report. Project vulnerabilities use the private route in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
