# Karatty v0.1.0-alpha.1

**English** · [Español](../es/releases/v0.1.0-alpha.1.md) · [Italiano](../it/releases/v0.1.0-alpha.1.md)

The first **experimental alpha for local use** offers an installed TUI, accepted 1:1 chats and end-to-end encryption. You need your own server/PostgreSQL; no project-hosted Internet service exists. No domain/VPS purchase or account email is needed for a local trial.

## Included

- Pseudonymous Ed25519 SSH-key registration without email/real name; recovery through an offline code.
- Requested/accepted direct chats, rejection/blocking and one device per account.
- Olm/vodozemac 0.11.1, fingerprints compared through another channel and re-verification after identity changes.
- Local-passphrase archive with Argon2id/XChaCha20-Poly1305, persisted outbox and exact-envelope retries after restart.
- Opaque PostgreSQL envelopes, sequences, 30-day retention/deduplication, account/local-file deletion.

## Known limits

- **Defender:** one server build was classified as `Behavior:Win32/DefenseEvasion.A!ml`. It worked after its owner restored it from quarantine, with protection active. A false positive/other installations have not been established. Do not disable protection to install.
- **Experimental LAN:** IP-SAN TLS configuration exists; two physical-machine chat has not been verified.
- **No independent Karatty audit.** The server sees metadata/can deny service; pseudonymity is not absolute anonymity.
- Forward secrecy excludes old state snapshots and unlocked/compromised devices. Account recovery cannot restore a missing archive/passphrase or never-decrypted messages.
- 16 MiB archive, 500 displayed messages and 100 listed conversations. Client cleanup needs use. Backups are not automatically configured; each administrator discloses/verifies their policy.
- Groups, attachments, multiple devices and remote SSH are outside this alpha. Legacy invitations store readable content and must not be treated as E2EE.
